Smart Future Cities India Expo

For the past decade, the Smart Future Cities India expo has endeavoured to build a better future by showcasing innovation & sustainability. The Smart Future Cities India expo integrates technology with the key pillars of urban development for optimising resources and making cities smart and sustainable. The 2026 expo will be a hub for future technologies, with a special focus on Robotics, Automation, Drones, Cyber Security, Urban Innovation, Smart Mobility, Smart ICT, Greentech and ESG.

The expo will showcase India’s emerging role as a leader in technology and a force for good in the world. As always, the expo will serve to amplify opportunities and provide a platform to engage with leaders from government and industry to chart a course for the future. The 2026 expo will focus on connecting smart city solution providers with senior government officials, industry leaders, Smart City CEOs, city planners, Mayors, Ambassadors and stakeholders from India and around the globe.

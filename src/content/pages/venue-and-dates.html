Expo Venue - Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi
Pragati Maidan, with an area of nearly 150 acres, is one of the biggest exhibition centers in India, and the ultimate venue for exhibitions and conventions in Delhi. The recently-refurbished state-of-the-art Integrated Exhibition cum Convention Centre (IECC) at Pragati Maidan serves as symbol of India’s growing might. The exhibition and convention industry in India and globally, is upbeat and enthusiastic about the upcoming complex. Over the years, the IECC will be a pivotal cog in the country’s trade & economic progress, and cement the nation’s stature as an emerging global superpower and knowledge hub.
